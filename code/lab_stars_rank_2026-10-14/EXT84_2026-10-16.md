## 1. Arithmetic: every rank-30 germ we have is excluded by DISC (PROVED + EXACT)
- DISC, re-derived here in full.  K = Z^84 n ker B has det 2^21 3^8 5^6 (det BB^T = 24 10^6 12^7, elementary
  divisors of B are 1^13 2).  On ker B: T = 3I - Gamma and U = 4I + Gamma are integral, T + U = 7, TU = 0, so
  7K is inside K_T + K_U and the index is a power of 7.  Integrality of the Gram matrices 8T and 15U forces
  det K_T = 2^21 7^a and det K_U = 3^8 5^6 7^b.  The label lattice of 8T at v is sqrt(56) pi_T(Z^84) = sqrt(56) K_T^*,
  with disc 56^30 / det K_T = 2^69 7^(30-a).
  Hence, for every set I of 30 labels at v with T_I nonsingular, the square class of det(8T_I) is 2 or 14.  The robust
  part (no lemma of Codex) is: no prime other than 2 and 7 has an odd exponent, and 2 has an odd exponent.
- Identity (PROVED, standard): class(Lambda_S) = class(pdet(T_SS) * det_std(N_S)), where N_S = Z^44 n ker T_SS.
  It was checked on both germs.
- Census (EXACT, disc_batch.py): 643 rank-30 PSD germs from the night's harvests, class representatives and urep runs,
  plus Codex's promoted HB.  ALL fail DISC robustly, with class 2*3*5 (3 germs, including urep100) or 2*3*5*7
  (the other 640 and HB).
- Spectral anatomy (EXACT, intmult.py and germ_spectrum.py).
  * Multiplicities of the integral eigenvalues of T_SS: m3 is 3 or 7 (always odd), m5 = 2 always, m7 is 0, 1 or 5.
  * 640 of the germs are h-invariant; the 3 urep bestpsd germs are not, and they show the same pattern.
- Grid mechanism (PROVED).  Suppose (l,m) spans a grid.  Then each corner {0,2}, {0,3}, {1,2}, {1,3} has as its 12
  neighbours 2 corners and 10 labels of O, and none in S outside the corners.  Hence:
  * kappa = e02 - e03 - e12 + e13 satisfies T kappa = 5 kappa;
  * v_l = (1_L1 - 1_L0) + 6(e02 + e03 - e12 - e13) and the analogous v_m satisfy B v = 0 and Gamma_SS v = 0, so
    T v = 3v;
  * these three vectors are orthogonal, with 8T-norms 160, 2880 and 2880, so their sublattice has class 10.
  So a grid germ passes DISC only if its orthogonal complement has class 5 or 35.
- Local mod 3 and mod 5 conditions.  15U mod p is independent of Gamma (p = 3, 5) and 8T 15U = 0, so a germ must
  satisfy 8T_SS g = 0 (mod p) for every g supported on S whose zero extension lies in col(15U mod p).  Computed:
  rank_3(15U) = 8 and rank_5(15U) = 6 on the 84 labels, matching v_3 = 32 and v_5 = 34 of disc Lambda_15U.  But
  dim Z_3(S) = 2 and dim Z_5(S) = 1, and both germs satisfy the condition.  Too weak; closed.

## 3. Link with (H-MATCH) through a third line (PROVED)
Take an h-related germ at (l,m) and a third line n.  The l-half of (H-MATCH) at (l,n) reads M_1 = s(n) M_0, while
M_1 = s(m) M_0 already holds.  So it holds iff M_0 is invariant under s(m)s(n); for a grid germ this means that M_0
pairs the two letters of n.
- If all three pairs among l, m, n are h-related, then M_0 and M_1 pair the four letters of m u n among themselves,
  M_2 and M_3 do so for l u n, and M_4 and M_5 for l u m.
- So every three-line extension of S towards a line n that M_0 does not pair produces a BROKEN (l,n) block, PSD of
  nullity >= 14: an exact counterexample to (H-MATCH).
- s6x.py tests this with Codex's columns.  For HB and urep100 every one of the 5 possible third lines is impossible
  (empty domains), and M_2 of HB pairs the letters of no outer line.

- LEMMA (h5) (PROVED).  No admissible structure on the stars 0,1,2,3,4 is invariant under h = (0 1)(2 3).
  Proof.  Let Y = {6,...,13} and x_y = {4,y} for y in Y.  Each x_y has exactly 2 neighbours in L_2 (C1: 2, 3 are not
  letters of x_y).  If x_y ~ {2,t} then x_y ~ h{2,t} = {3,h(t)}.
  t = 4 is impossible: x_y would have the two neighbours {2,4}, {3,4} in L_4, while C1 allows exactly one.
  So t is in {0, 1, 5} u Y, and x_y is a common neighbour of the pair P_t = ({2,t},{3,h(t)}).
  * For t in {5} u Y the two labels share the letter t, so C2 allows at most 1 common label neighbour.
  * For t in {0,1} they are disjoint corners ({2,0},{3,1} and {2,1},{3,0}), so C2 allows at most 2.
  Distinct neighbours give distinct pairs.  So the 8 x 2 = 16 incidences fit into at most 9 x 1 + 2 x 2 = 13 places.
  Contradiction.
  Scope: this excludes h-invariant five-star EXTENSIONS.  An h-invariant four-star germ could a priori extend in a
  non-symmetric way.  The SAT runs above show that the h-invariant germs on file do not extend at all.

## 7. Codex's quantitative strengthening of (h5) (his result; I checked the argument)

assumed).  It also holds for L_0 against L_1.  In general D >= 2k - 16.
