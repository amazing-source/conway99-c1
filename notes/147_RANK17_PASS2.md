## What is tested per (V, y-pair)

It is a necessary condition for the frame, so failing it excludes the pair. For every skeleton g₂..g₇ that passes the class
filters (146, pass 1, items 1–2), the 21 cell domains must admit 42 codewords q = (r | w) that are pairwise orthogonal mod 4.
- The cell domains are SPEC §1: in-class rule (w⁺,w⁻) ∈ {0,±4}, w⁻ ≠ ±w⁺; M1; refined L1; sign normalisation of a₁₂, b₁₂, b₁d.
- The integer rule (w_X,w_Y) = 4(u,v) − r_X·r_Y implies the mod-4 rule.
- By structures' ℤ₄ algebra (conway99-rank17 z17/z4test.py; `GPT_FOLLOWUP_MOD4.md`), the codewords fit iff they lie in one
  self-dual ℤ₄ code whose residue is the graph of ρ. Equivalently, there is φ with rows M₂..M₇ (21 free bits) satisfying:
  - M_c[d] + M_d[c] = (b_c,b_d)/2;
  - φ(d₁d) = M_d ∈ Σ₁d;
  - φ(d_cd) = M_c + M_d + κ_cd ∈ Σ_cd.
- Σ_X is the set of signatures σ(q⁺) of the admissible cell entries. My general-V form uses the U′ Gram I₇ ⊕ G_V:
  - s(q) = ((q − q mod 2) mod 4)/2;
  - σ(q)_e = (s, b_e)_{U′}, with b_e = (e₁ + e_e | g_e mod 2V);
  - κ_cd[e] = (b̃_c ∗ b̃_d, b̃_e) mod 2.

**Derived facts used by the engine** (DERIVED; checked numerically, see Validation):
1. For w in a class γ, the pairing (s(w), g) mod 2 is linear in g ∈ K/2K. It is given by the 15-bit functional
   f(x) = (x G_V K_Vᵀ) mod 2. So a cell entry is summarised by three functionals: f_s(w⁺), f_s(w⁻), and f_a of a = (w⁺+w⁻)/2.
2. **M1 ⟺ σ(q⁺) = σ(q⁻), bit by bit.**
   - f_a = f_rep ⊕ f_s(w⁺) ⊕ f_s(w⁻).
   - pattern(f_rep of the class of cell (c,d)) = ε_c + e_d. This uses that the g's pair oddly with each other and are even
     with themselves (norm 10).
3. **Partial test (necessary condition).** Take k chosen classes (k = 3, 4, 5) and the cells among labels 1..k+1:
   - read M1 and the signatures on the k known bits;
   - skip refined L1 (it needs all six bits);
   - restrict the rows and κ, J to those bits.
   Then every Σ is a superset of the projection of the true Σ, so any full solution projects to a partial one. If the partial
   test fails, no completion survives.
4. **Canonical enumeration under H = W(R_K).** H fixes y₁ and y₂; every filter and the vector level are H-equivariant.
   - Level j carries G_j ≤ H. G_j fixes x₁..x_j and descends: G_{j+1} is generated by random Schreier generators of
     Stab_{G_j}(x_{j+1}), which is a subgroup of the stabiliser, so the chain stays valid.
   - A set S is enumerated as (x₁..x₆), where x_{j+1} is the minimum of S ∖ {x₁..x_j} in the order (G_j-orbit id, index),
     and x_{j+1} must be a G_j-orbit minimum.
   - Every H-orbit of skeletons has such a representative: apply elements of G₀, G₁, … level by level. Each one fixes the
     earlier x's and preserves the earlier orbit ids.
   - Labels follow the enumeration order. The S₆ relabelling and the h-flips are symmetries of the relaxation, so any
     labelling is valid.

## Faster engine (commit c906646)

- **Stabilizer chain restricted to the candidate sets.** Cand_j is G_j-invariant, because it is cut out by adjacency to
  fixed points and by the earlier (orbit id, index) orders; the program asserts this at run time.
  - Default depth 6.
  - H-invariant fingerprints are identical to the plain enumeration on 3 sub-instances.
  - 6–16× fewer leaves.
- **Incremental partial test.**
  - Old cells are filtered by the new bit; new cells are scanned.
  - Node, test and prune counts are identical to the non-incremental version; about 2× faster.
- **Leaf pre-test:** the same incremental test at 6 bits, without L1 (a relaxation).
- **Classification mode (driver):** a pair is EXCLUDED, or MOD4_FEASIBLE when the search stops at its first mod-4 survivor.

## Why the mod-4 relaxation is not enough there (A11E6⁺ #0, PATTERN-level diagnostics)

- **Scale.** The first top-level branch alone has more than 1.7×10⁷ canonical leaves after partial pruning, and 27% of the
  leaves pass the full mod-4 test.
- **Integer solver on survivors.** Every mod-4 survivor tested (12) dies in sbdfs_g at star-1 depth 3, at 3–19 s each.
  Exhaustion would therefore take about 10⁸ s per pair: infeasible.
- **φ-restriction.** Restrict every cell to the entries whose signature is φ(d_X), for one mod-4 solution φ.
  - Then sbdfs_g dies at the root (1–85 nodes).
  - There are 96–1,368 φ per skeleton.
  - First wipeouts over 3,288 (skeleton, φ) cases:
    - 91% are between two star-1 cells;
    - most often both cells have a single entry.
  - Reading: φ fixes the unimodular U. Each star-1 cell then has almost no norm-3 vector u ∈ U with (u,h₁) = 1 and
    (u,h_d) = ±1. The ones that exist pair to |(u,v)| = 2 instead of ≤ 1.
  - The obstruction is route (C)'s star-1 problem inside that U.
- **No early pruning found.** None of these tests kills on partial skeletons:
  - the integer CSP on the cells among labels 1..k+1 (k = 3, 4: feasible in 7 and 11 nodes);
  - the hybrid test (partial φ × integer arc consistency; k = 5: 828 of 984 partial φ alive);
  - the frame energy along short duals κ ∈ K* (A11E6⁺ #0 has a single one, |κ|² = 3/7; the minima sum to 0 < 6).
  The kill needs the last label: it is global.
- **Consequence.** The MOD4_FEASIBLE pairs stay PATTERN (pass-1 evidence plus these diagnostics) until a lemma exists.
  C2's frame energy is the candidate. The star-1-in-U reading above is its natural target.
